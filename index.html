Swyli

Clean your photos, contacts and Gmail inbox.

Swyli is an iPhone app. Sort photos and videos with a swipe, merge duplicate contacts, and clear promotional email from your Gmail inbox. You review everything before it is deleted, and your data is processed on your iPhone. If you connect Gmail, Swyli lists your email by category, moves what you choose to Gmail Trash and helps you unsubscribe. Your email never reaches a Swyli server.
